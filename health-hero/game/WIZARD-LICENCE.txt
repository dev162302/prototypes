CHARACTER ART — PROVENANCE AND LICENCE
======================================

Asset      wizard.png — five-frame run cycle
Source     Supplied by the client's designer for this campaign
           ("Sprite Sheet.svg", 9500x2176, delivered 2026-08-16)
Licence    Client-owned campaign artwork. Not third-party, not stock.

Prepared from the delivered SVG rather than used as sent:
  - rasterised at 14.264 screen px per art pixel, which is the ratio the art
    is actually drawn at, giving a clean ~102px-tall pixel grid
  - the five poses put on a common baseline (bottoms varied by 47px of 1450)
  - each pose centred on its torso, not its bounding box, so the figure sits
    on its collision circle rather than swimming against it

The previous placeholder — "wizard character (animated)" by ruberboy,
OpenGameArt, CC0 — has been removed along with bull.png and disc.png, which
the Novo orb replaced. Nothing third-party remains in this directory.
