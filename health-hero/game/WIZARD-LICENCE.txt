WHAT CC0 DOES NOT GIVE YOU
--------------------------
CC0 is a dedication made by the uploader, not a warranty. It is only valid if
the uploader actually held the rights, and OpenGameArt does not verify that.
There is no indemnity behind it — unlike a paid stock licence, nobody carries
the risk for you if the provenance turns out to be wrong.

Nothing here suggests a problem with this particular asset. But for a public
campaign for a pharmaceutical client, "probably fine, no warranty" is a
decision for the client's legal team to take knowingly, not one to inherit by
default. Declare it.
