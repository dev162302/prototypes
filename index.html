Prototypes

A selected set of nine. All single-file, no dependencies, no install — open one on a phone and it runs.

RIPPLE RUN · TIME TRIAL The speedrun cut. No ripple at all — not as a mechanic, not as an effect. What's scarce is the jump: you start with four, every jump spends one, and each pill gives one back — and there are only two or three spare across a whole level. Every level sets a jump par and a target time; three ticks needs both. Arrow gates give a boost, the rotating star takes a second off the clock, and a bird outruns you the whole way. Ranked on time first, fewer jumps breaking a tie. Canvas 2D · 40KB · portrait RIPPLE RUN The platformer, branded. Every tap jumps; a tap in the air sends a ripple, and everything it reaches sends one of its own — each step outward scores more than the last. Brick walls can only be rippled through. Nine levels. Canvas 2D · 39KB · portrait RIPPLE The platformer. Ripple dormant rings into a bridge, then move before it fades. Four stages, a boss, a 90-second Daily Run and a leaderboard. Canvas 2D · 96 KB · the link already shared with the client QUEUE BREAK Editorial top-down tennis against Boredom, first to seven. Drag anywhere to move your racket; the rally speeds up the longer you hold on. Canvas 2D · 15 KB · ?demo for a 2-point match ORB KINGDOM Base-builder idle. Orbs arrive every second — recruit an army, raise walls, upgrade camp to citadel, repel a boredom wave every few minutes. DOM · 35 KB · ?demo for a fast first wave WAITING GAMEV1 Stop the loader in the gate. Dead centre pays double. The original timing prototype. Canvas 2D · 18 KB FIVE SECONDSV1 Five seconds, count the taps. The Wolt hidden-game clone, rebuilt on brand. DOM · 14 KB ORB CLICKER Cookie-Clicker structure in three columns. Tap the orb, hire ten generators, catch the magenta bonus for a ×7 frenzy. DOM · 30 KB · offline earnings HABIT HERO Eight self-care habits fill their own bars and pay out. Tap to hurry one along, level it up to double it. DOM · 12 KB

Warps for demos. RIPPLE RUN takes ?lvl=2 to open a chosen level and ?lvl=2&x=270 to drop in partway — ?lvl=2 is the first brick wall. RIPPLE takes #run=1 for a Daily Run, #board=1 for the leaderboard and #stage=3&x=1500 for a point inside a stage. QUEUE BREAK and ORB KINGDOM take ?demo for a short round.

Orientation. RIPPLE RUN is portrait and frames itself as a phone on a desktop screen. RIPPLE is landscape. The rest are portrait-first — hold the phone upright.

Scores are stored on the device only. No accounts, no tracking, no install.

Still placeholders. Logo, exact brand blues and the typeface are all standing in until the guidelines pack arrives. Nothing here is an official brand asset.
